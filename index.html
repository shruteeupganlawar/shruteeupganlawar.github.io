Industry & Research Internships & Roles

I'm a manufacturing engineer turned roboticist — one who's worked across varied domains, from automobile assembly lines at Bajaj Auto Ltd. and Mercedes-Benz, researching aircraft components and 3D printing at Skyroot Aerospace, to working on communication protocols in robotic systems and using LLMs for zero-shot trajectory generation at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.

My aim lies in giving robots a nervous system — the ability to understand the world they inhabit — building machines that perceive, reason, and adapt in real time. I aspire to dive deep into SLAM, motion planning, robot cognition, and perception to build autonomous systems that navigate and reason in unstructured environments.

Beyond research and engineering, I've led from the front — as Secretary of TEDxCOEP, curating the most talks in the club's history, and as a student representative on the Board of Studies shaping the undergraduate curriculum for Manufacturing Science and Engineering at COEP. I believe the same systems thinking that makes a good roboticist makes a good leader: define the problem, align the team, iterate.

In my world parallel with robotics, you can find me writing poems, reflections, musings, exploring nerdy topics (especially astronomy) or dancing!

03 — Projects

Things I've Built

Language Models as Zero-Shot Trajectory Generators

Mitigating execution failures via hybrid learning — Constraint-Aware Inverse Kinematics, Synchronized Gripper Control, and Perception Feedback. Made the RGB-D camera pipeline robust for smoother, more reliable robot motions.

CFRP + GFRP Integration for Payload Fairing Design

Investigated hybrid composite materials for spacecraft payload fairings — targeting cost reduction while maintaining structural integrity. Validated via ANSYS Fluent, structural & modal analysis using layered surface feature for LEO missions.

UV-Based Bone Mineral Density Measurement

Prototype device using UV rays, mirrors, diffraction gratings, and photodetectors to compute BMD — a low-cost, non-invasive alternative to DEXA and QUS scans for osteoporosis detection.

Throat Disease Detection via Infrared Thermography + CNN

Non-invasive disease detection using a CNN model to identify illness-related temperature anomalies in the throat region through infrared thermographic imaging.

Gravity Simulator Web App

Interactive web application allowing users to manipulate celestial body mass and orbital distance from the Sun, visualizing resulting trajectory and orbital changes in real time.
